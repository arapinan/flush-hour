Flush Hour

Finds public restrooms in New York City that are open when you get there.

Ask where to go

Tell it where you are, or turn on your location. It searches the city's official list of about 1,000 public restrooms, checks their hours for the time you'd arrive, and can plan stops along a walk. Mention what you need (wheelchair access, a changing station, an all-gender restroom) and it remembers for the rest of the chat.
